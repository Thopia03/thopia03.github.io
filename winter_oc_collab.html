📜 Collab Rules & Guidelines

Ruleset:

The rules are overall the same as in the posting:

  • Draw your OC(s): The main aspect of this collaboration are your OCs! However, you are allowed to also draw other characters (from other existing universes, e.g., a Genshin OC), as long as your character(s) are still inside the artwork!
  • Winter theme: Since this is a 'winter' collab, it is obvious that the artwork stays true to it. However, it is your decision in which way you realize this: a cozy evening in front of the chimney? Or an epic battle in the snow? Perhaps a nice walk in a snowy forest? It's up to you!
  • No Tracing
  • No NSFW: Since I plan for us to post this collab on Instagram, I'd like everybody to keep this SFW. While I don't think a little blood, a slight depiction of violence, or a little suggestive tone shouldn't be a problem, just remember where we're posting this collab. If you are generally unsure if your idea complies with this rule, I strongly advise you to ask!
  • NO AI: I don't think I need to say much about this. We are a workspace of human creativity.

WIPs:

After gaining some questions regarding the WIPs of your artworks, I'd like to answer them here:

  • Sending your progress via sketches, time-lapses, etc. is no necessity for the collab! But you're always free to share.
  • You are allowed to share your WIPs with your followers, and I even encourage you to do so!

Canvas Specs:

There aren't many rules I want to impose, but I generally want a uniform way for my templates ^^

  • Dimensions: A4 vertically (210mm x 297mm)
  • File Type: Transparent .PNG

(However, if you want to draw horizontally, we might be able to accommodate this, based on how many spaces are occupied on the last day people can join or how many other people want to make a horizontal piece - maybe ask around in the group chat! c:)
